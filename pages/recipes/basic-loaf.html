Basic (Workhorse) Loaf

Category: Bread

Status: Tested

This bread is the basis to every loaf

In our house, I used to make this loaf so often it earned the name "Daily Loaf".

With a little adjustment, it manages practically everything: small or large loaves, crusty or soft buns, it makes great chunky toast, cob, garlic dough balls, focaccia, cinnamon rolls!

Ingredients

  • 2 C Strong White Flour, plus extra for sprinkling
  • 1 t Salt
  • 1 t Yeast
  • 1 C Water, warm

Method

Prep Time: 2hr 30min

Temperature: 200

Cook Time: 25min

Servings: 1 Small Loaf

  • 2 C Flour
  • 1 t Salt
  • 1 t Yeast
 Combine dry ingredients in bowl.

Keep Salt from touching Yeast until mixed with Flour.
  • 1 C Water, Warm
 Add Water and mix thoroughly, until uniform and sticky.

The handle of a wooden spoon works well for this stiff mixing.
Clean sides bowl and mixing utensil with a silicone spatula.

Knead dough 7min, until workable and less sticky.

Transfer dough to bowl, cover, rise for 45min.
  • 6" Loaf Tin, greased or lined
  • Flour, for sprinkling
 With the drier "skin" of the dough on the work-surface, deflate and flatten into isosceles triangle where the base is equal to the length of the loaf tin.

Then roll up from point to base, pressing to bind dough together, forming a log.

Place in loaf tin, sprinkle Flour over dough.

Gently cover tin with clingfilm or cloth, prove for 45min.
Peel off clingfilm, bake at 200 for 25min.

Wait 5min for loaf to firm in tin before transferring to a cooling rack.

Variations

Crusty Buns

  1. Same procedure up to Stage 5.
  2. Then divide into 8 portions, shape into balls with tension across upper skin, sprinkle with flour and prove 45min.
  3. Then peel clingfilm and bake at 200 for 15min.

Simple Focaccia: Olive Oil, Fresh Thyme, Flaked Salt

  1. Spread 1 t oil over dough ball and the sides of the bowl immediately before each rise.
  2. Prepare by spreading dough to 1/2" thickness with fingertips over a large sheet pan, randomly poke with fingertips to create indentations all over the dough sheet, and drizzle with oil.
  3. Allow to prove 45min.
  4. Drizzle with more oil, sprinkle with seasonings, and bake at 200 for 15min.

Chef's Notes

Personally, I line the bowls with a little oil to make decanting a little easier.

If you are going to score the surface of the loaf, do so in Stage 6, after its final rise, immediately before baking.
