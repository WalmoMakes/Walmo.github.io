Pomp's Butter Loaf

Category: Bread

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 460g Strong White Flour, plus extra for sprinkling
  • 60g Butter
  • 1 (7g) Packet Dried Yeast
  • 1 T Salt
  • 1 T White Sugar
  • 250ml Water, warm

Method

Prep Time: 1hr 40min

Temperature: 220

Cook Time: 35min

Servings: 1 Small Loaf

  • 460g Flour
  • 60g Butter
 Combine Flour and Butter in bowl.

Rub into breadcrumb texture.
  • 1 Packet Yeast
  • 1 T Salt
  • 1 T Sugar
 Add remaining dry ingredients to flour mixture.

Keep Yeast separated from Salt until combined with flour mixture.
  • 250ml Water, warm
 Add half the Water, combine, then add up to the remaining half, and combine.
Knead dough for 5min until smooth and elastic.

Return to bowl, cover, and rise 45min.
  • 6" Loaf Tin, greased or lined
  • Flour, for sprinkling
 Degas dough and knead for 30sec.

Shape, and transfer to Tin.

Sprinkle with flour, cover, and prove 45min.
Uncover and bake at 220 for 35min, until medium-brown.
