Ciabatta

Category: Bread

Status: Tested

These make for great, chewy sandwiches.

Ingredients

  • 400g Strong White Flour
  • 2 t Salt
  • 1 t White Sugar
  • ½ t Yeast
  • 300ml Water, warm

Method

Prep Time: 2hr 30min

Temperature: 250+

Cook Time: 18-23min

Servings: 4x 6" Buns

  • 400g Flour
  • 2 t Salt
  • 1 t Sugar
  • ½ t Yeast
  • 304ml Water, warm
 Pour the Water into a large bowl, then add approximately half of the Flour, then the Salt, Sugar, Yeast in a large bowl.

Stir to combine, until batter consistency.

Add remaining Flour and stir with a silicone spatula until a very wet and sticky dough forms.
Divide dough into 4 smaller oiled bowls.

Rise 2hr, covered, until doubled.
  • Flour, for sprinkling
  • Baking Tray
  • Silicone Mat
 Sprinkle a generous quantity of flour onto a large baking tray and a silicone mat.

Pour one portion of dough onto floured mat, sprinkle with more flour.

Raise mat edges to shape dough into a flat 6" loaf.

Roll loaf off mat and onto baking tray.

Repeat until all 4 loaves are on the tray.
  • Steamed Oven
 Bake loaves in a steamed oven at max temperature for 8min, then turn the oven down to 200, and bake for another 10-15min until golden-brown.

Transfer to a cooling rack immediately and brush off excess flour.
