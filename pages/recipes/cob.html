Cob (Dutch oven)

Category: Bread

Status: Tested

This loaf is very easy to make thanks to a bread-making technique called autolyse, and even though we're not using the technique properly, it still reduces the amount of kneading we have to do from about 10min down to 3min.

Ingredients

  • 2 C Strong White Flour
  • 1 t Salt
  • 1 t Yeast
  • 1 C Water, warm

Method

Prep Time: 1hr 45min

Temperature: 200

Cook Time: 40min

Servings: 1 Small Loaf

  • 2 C Flour
  • 1 t Salt
  • 1 t Yeast
 Combine dry ingredients in bowl.

Keep Salt from touching Yeast until mixed with Flour.
  • 1 C Water
 Add Water and mix thoroughly, until uniform and sticky.

The handle of a wooden spoon works well for this stiff mixing.
Scrape any remaining dough from the sides of the bowl and mixing utensil.

Cover bowl, rise for 45min.
  • Lidded Dutch Oven
 Knead for 3min until elastic, form into ball.

Clean, dry and line bowl with baking paper.

Place dough into lined bowl, cover and prove for 45min.

Preheat Dutch Oven and Lid at 200.
Remove Dutch Oven from oven, remove lid, and gently transfer baking paper sling from bowl to Dutch Oven, aiming to not deflate the dough.

Immediately place lid back on and return Dutch Oven to oven.
Bake at 200 for 30min, then remove Lid, and bake 5-10min more, until loaf crust reaches desired colour.
Remove Dutch Oven from oven, remove loaf from Dutch Oven.

Peel baking paper from loaf, transfer loaf to cooling rack.

Chef's Notes

This loaf can be baked until the crust is very dark, and still be tasty.

It's not burnt, it's a "well-developed crust."
