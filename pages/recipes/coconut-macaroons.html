Coconut Macaroons

Category: Cake

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 200g Desiccated Coconut (unsweetened)
  • 135g Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 1 t Caster Sugar
  • ½ t Vanilla Extract
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 2 Egg Whites

Method

Prep Time: 5min

Temperature: 190

Cook Time: 15min

Servings: 9 Macaroons

  • 200g Desiccated Coconut
  • 135g Condensed Milk
  • 1 t Sugar
  • ½ t Vanilla Extract
  • Pinch of Salt
 Using a wooden spoon, combine Coconut, Condensed Milk, Sugar, Vanilla, Salt in a large bowl.

Mixture should be sticky and clumping, but not wet.
  • 2 Egg Whites
 In a separate large bowl, whisk Egg Whites until stiff peaks form.
Fold Egg mix into Coconut mix.

Mixture should be damp and hold a shape: not all whipped egg may be required to achieve this.
Using an ice-cream scoop, scoop the mixture onto a baking sheet lined with baking paper.

Aim for uniform balls with few flakes protruding, as these will darken before the rest of the macaroons, and risk burning.

Wet finger to flatten tops slightly, and reshape any, if necessary.
Bake 10min, rotate the tray of macaroons to encourage even colouring.

Return to oven 5min until flake edges and around base are golden.
Transfer to wire rack until warm and either consume now or wait until cooled completely, then store, covered.

Variations

Chocolate Dipped: 50g Milk or Dark Chocolate

  1. Once baked, and cooled fully, proceed.
  2. Melt and temper 50g Chocolate in a bowl.
  3. Dip cooled Macaroons in melted Chocolate until base and 1cm up sides.
  4. Place on baking sheet and refrigerate 10min, until Chocolate solid.

Chocolate Orange Surprise: ¼ Terry's Chocolate Orange

  1. Snap Chocolate segments in half.
  2. Press into balls during Stage 4, ensure Coconut mix sealed over Chocolate.
  3. Bake as usual.

Chef's Notes

When forming the macaroons, dip the ice-cream scoop in water between every couple macaroons, to discourage the mixture from sticking to the scoop.
