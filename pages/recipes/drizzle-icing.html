Drizzle Icing

Category: Icings, Sweets, Desserts, Puddings

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 1 C (125g) Icing Sugar
  • 1-3 T Water or Milk, warm

Method

Prep Time: 2min

Temperature: N/A

Cook Time: N/A

Servings: Enough to coat 12 biscuits

  • 1 C Icing Sugar
  • 1-3 T Liquid, warm
 Sift the Icing Sugar into a bowl and add the Water, 1 t at a time, then stir to combine.
Add more Water until the icing becomes thick enough to coat the back of a spoon (appx. 3 t, aka. 1 T)

If you would prefer a flood icing, try up to 3 T liquid.
