Flammkuchen

Category: Unleavened

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 300g Plain Flour
  • 1 ½ t Salt
  • 30g Butter, softened
  • 185g Water

Method

Prep Time: 30min

Temperature: 250+

Cook Time: 7-10min

Servings: 2 Trays

  • 300g Flour
  • 1 ½ t Salt
  • 30g Butter, softened
 Mix Salt into Flour.

Rub Butter into Flour mix until combined.
  • 185g Water
 Add Water to Flour mix and stir to combine into a soft, slightly sticky dough.
  • Flour, for sprinkling
 Transfer to a floured surface and knead briefly until homogeneous.

Wrap dough in clingfilm and refrigerate 20min.

If cooking immediately, bring oven to max temperature.
  • Flour, for sprinkling
 alf dough, sprinkle with Flour and roll dough into a rectangle approximately the size of a baking sheet and 1-2mm thick.

Brush off excess Flour, then transfer to a lined or greased baking sheet.
Top with toppings, leaving some room around the edge to form a crust.

Transfer to oven and bake 7-10min, until bread bubbles and chars.

Variations

Traditional: ¼ C Yellow Onion, thinly sliced, ¼ C Leek, sliced, 15g Cured Ham, cubed, ½ C Crème fraîche, ½ t Salt, Pinch Ground Black Pepper, Pinch Ground Nutmeg

  1. Stir Salt, Pepper, Nutmeg into Crème fraîche.
  2. Liberally spread mix onto unbaked flammkuchen.
  3. Sprinkle with Onion, Leek, Ham.
  4. Bake as usual.

Chef's Notes

Wood-burning ovens take a while to heat and get very hot at first, before becoming more stable as they gradually cool.

Traditionally, flammkuchen (flame-tart) are the first things cooked in a hot oven while it is still at its peak, as a way of testing the temperature, and not wasting any hard-earned heat.
