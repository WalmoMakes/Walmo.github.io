Flapjack

Category: Biscuit

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 150g Butter
  • 100g Soft Light Brown Sugar
  • 90g Golden Syrup
  • 200g Porridge Oats (not quick oats)

Method

Prep Time: 5min

Temperature: 180

Cook Time: 15-20min

Servings: 8-12 Servings

  • 150g Butter
  • 100g Sugar
  • 90g Golden Syrup
 Melt Butter, Sugar, Golden Syrup over low flame in a small pan, or in a small bowl in the microwave.
  • 200g Oats
 In a large bowl, pour Butter mix over Oats, stir to combine.
  • 6x6" Shallow Cake Tin, lined
 Press Oat mix firmly into a lined 6x6" Cake Tin.

Press corners in first and last, as they tend to bulge while pressing.
Bake 180 for 15-20min, until golden-brown, and centre wobbles slightly.
Cool in tin until warm (appx. 15min), then cut into 8-12 pcs, return to tin using lining as a sling, or transfer to a cooling rack if solid enough.

Fully decant when completely cool, appx. 1hr.

Variations

Rasin: 85g Raisins

  1. Pour boiling water over Raisins in a small bowl, leave to rehydrate for 5min, drain.
  2. Add rehydrated Raisins to Oat mixture in Stage 2.

Apricot / Cherry and WHite Chocolate: 50g Dried Apricots OR Candied Cherries, chopped, 50g White Chocolate chunks, divided

  1. Add Apricot / Cherries and 20g Chocolate Chunks to Oat mixture in Stage 2.
  2. Melt remaining 30g Chocolate, and drizzle over cooled Flapjack.

Date and Walnut: 25g Walnuts, chopped, 50g Dates, stoned

  1. Rehydrate Dates using same method as for Raisins, shown above.
  2. Add Walnuts and rehydrated Dates to Oat mixture in Stage 2.
