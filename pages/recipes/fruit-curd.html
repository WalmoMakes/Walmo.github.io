Fruit Curd

Category: Jams

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 2 Large Citrus, Juice, and finely grated Zest
  • 145g Butter, diced
  • 125g Caster Sugar
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 2 Eggs, room temperature, beaten

Method

Prep Time: 15min

Temperature: 150

Cook Time: 50min

Servings: 8 Servings

  • Citrus Juice and Zest
  • 145g Butter, diced
  • 125g Sugar
  • Pinch of Salt
 Wash Citrus, then juice and zest them into a Saucepan.

Add Butter and Sugar to juice.
Heat mixture over low heat, constantly stirring with silicone spatula until sugar completely dissolved.
  • 2 Eggs, room temperature, beaten
  • Candy Thermometer
 Strain beaten Eggs into mixture, then stir constantly until the mixture becomes very thick, opaque, and passes the spoon test.

Read appx. 74-77degC on a candy thermometer, appx. 20min.
Remove from heat, transfer to sterilised jars.
Curd is ready when cooled to room temperature, may thicken overnight.

Variations

Citrus: ingredient1, ingredient2

  • Lemon: 2 large Lemons, Juice and finely grated Zest.
  • Orange and Ginger: 2 Large Oranges, Juice and finely grated Zest, 1" fresh Ginger, minced.

Soft Fruits: ingredient1, ingredient2

  • Blackcurrant: ⅓ C Blackcurrants, mashed.
  • Raspberry: ⅓ C Raspberries, mashed.
  • Sour Cherry: ½ C Cherries, mashed.
  • Pineapple: ½ C Pineapple, mashed.

Dried Fruits: ingredient1, ingredient2

  • Apricot: ½ C dried Apricots, finely diced, then left to rehydrate overnight in ½ C Water.
