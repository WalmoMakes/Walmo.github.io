BCHS' Ginger Parkin

Category: Cake

Status: Tested

As part of an open day at BCHS, I was put to work in the food tech classrooms, baking batch after batch of parkin and marbled pear upside-down cake.

I think I made about 8 batches that night, totalling appx. 100 servings.

I don't remember there being any leftovers.

Ingredients

  • 225g Self-Raising Flour
  • 110g White Sugar
  • 1 ½ t Ground Ginger
  • 1 t Baking Soda
  • 55g Butter
  • 110g Golden Syrup
  • 200ml Milk
  • 1 Egg

Method

Prep Time: 10min

Temperature: 200

Cook Time: 30-35min

Servings: 12 Servings

  • 225g SR Flour
  • 110g Sugar
  • 1 ½ t Ground Ginger
  • 1 t Baking Soda
 In a large bowl, combine Flour, Sugar, Ginger, Baking Soda.
  • 55g Butter
  • 110g Golden Syrup
 In a pan, melt Butter and Syrup.
  • 200ml Milk
  • 1 Egg
 In a bowl, beat together Egg and Milk.
Pour syrup mix into flour mix, stir to combine.

Mixture will be dry.
Add egg mix to flour mix, stir to Combine.

Mixture will be runny.
  • Lined 8x8" Tin
 Pour into tin, bake at 200 for 30-35min.

Variations

Jamaican Ginger Cake: Substitute half the Golden Syrup for Treacle, and add 1 t freshly grated Ginger to the butter mixture in Stage 2.
