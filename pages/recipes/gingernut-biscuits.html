Gingernut Biscuits

Category: Biscuit

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 125g White Sugar
  • 125g Soft Light Brown Sugar
  • 125g Butter
  • 1 T Golden Syrup
  • 1 T Fresh Ginger, finely grated
  • 250g Plain Flour
  • 1 T Baking Powder
  • 1/2 t Baking Soda
  • 1 T Ground Ginger
  • 1 T Ground Cinnamon
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 1 Egg

Method

Prep Time: 45min

Temperature: 160

Cook Time: 13min

Servings: 24 Biscuits

  • 125g White Sugar
  • 125g LB Sugar
  • 125g Butter
  • 1 T Syrup
  • 1 T Ginger, grated
 Combine Sugars, Syrup, Butter and Fresh Ginger into Saucepan, melt over medium heat, stirring.

Alternatively, melt these ingredients together in a small bowl, in the microwave.

Once melted and combined, remove from heat, set aside to cool a little.
  • 250g Flour
  • 1 T Baking Powder
  • 1/2 t Baking Soda
  • 1 T Ground Ginger
  • 1 T Ground Cinnamon
  • Pinch of Salt
 Combine the remaining ingredients in a large bowl.
  • 1 Egg
 Pour Sugar mix into Flour mix, combine until homogeneous and stiff.

Stir Egg into mixture until homogeneous and wet, then refrigerate 30min, until mixture is firm.
LiLightly oil hands, then roll 1 T mixture into balls, and space on a lined baking tray, giving appx. 2" space between balls.
Bake 160 for 7min, then remove tray from oven, smack tray against something flat and solid (like a chopping board or the bench) to degas cookies, then return to oven 6min.

Prep a second tray while this one is in the oven.

NB These biscuits burn very quickly.
Cool 5min on tray before transferring to cooling rack.

Variations

This recipe is for chewy biscuits. If you want crunchy biscuits skip the 30min refrigeration and instead, add 1/2 cup of flour (appx. 60g), beat to combine, then bake for 1-2min less after degassing them.
