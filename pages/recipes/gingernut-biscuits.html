Gingernut Biscuits

Category: Biscuit

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 125g White Sugar
  • 125g Soft Light Brown Sugar
  • 125g Butter
  • 1 T Golden Syrup
  • 1 T Fresh Ginger, finely grated
  • 250g Plain Flour
  • 1 T Baking Powder
  • 1/2 t Baking Soda
  • 1 T Ground Ginger
  • 1 T Ground Cinnamon
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 1 Egg

Method

Prep Time: 45min

Temperature: 160

Cook Time: 15min

Servings: 12-24 Biscuits

  • 125g White Sugar
  • 125g LB Sugar
  • 125g Butter
  • 1 T Syrup
  • 1 T Ginger, grated
 Combine Sugars, Syrup, Butter and Fresh Ginger into Saucepan, melt over medium heat, stirring.

Alternatively, melt these ingredients together in a small bowl, in the microwave.

Once melted and combined, remove from heat, set aside to cool a little.
  • 250g Flour
  • 1 T Baking Powder
  • 1/2 t Baking Soda
  • 1 T Ground Ginger
  • 1 T Ground Cinnamon
  • Pinch of Salt
 Combine the remaining ingredients in a large bow
  • Ingredient
 Pour Sugar mix into Flour mix, combine until homogeneous and stiff.

Stir Egg into mixture until homogeneous, then refrigerate 30min.
Roll 1 T mixture into balls, and space on baking tray, giving appx. 2" space between balls.
Bake 160 for 7min, then remove tray from oven, smack tray against bench to degas cookies, then return to oven 8min.
Cool 5min on tray before transferring to cooling rack.
