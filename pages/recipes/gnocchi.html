Gnocchi

Category: Miscellany

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 250g Potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 35g Plain Flour
  • ½ Egg Yolk
  • ½ t Salt, plus extra for water seasoning

Method

Prep Time: 10min

Temperature: High

Cook Time: 2-5min

Servings: 2-3 Servings

  • 250g Potatoes
  • 35g Flour
  • ½ Egg Yolk
  • ½ t Salt
 Combine all ingredients, folding gently with hands.

Take care not to overwork dough, or it will get sticky and dense.
Divide equally, roll into logs ½" diameter.

Cut into 1" lengths, shape if desired, and roll in flour.

Rolling in flour may best be performed on a large tray with a lip and moving the tray.
  • Salt for water seasoning
 Bring a pot of water to boil, add Salt.

Add gnocchi to boiling water, once they float (appx. 2-5min), remove them with a slotted spoon, and place them in a colander to drain.
Optionally, additionally fry in butter, or fry then roast.

Variations

Quick Gnocchi: Replace Whole Potatoes with Dehydrated Potato Flakes, rehydrated.

Leftovers Gnocchi: Add enough Plain Flour to leftover, seasoned, mashed Potato to barely bring it together into a dough.

Sage Butter and Cheese: Knob of Butter, Soft Cheese, sliced, Handful of fresh Sage leaves.

  1. Melt a large knob of Butter in an oven and hob-safe pan, add a handful of Sage leaves, soften leaves 30sec.
  2. Add drained Gnocchi, toss to coat in sage butter, then place slices of Soft Cheese over Gnocchi.
  3. Cook at 220 for 15min, uncovered, until blistering and crispy.

Mediterranean Vegetables: ingredient1, ingredient2

  1. Marinade Vegetables 1hr, then roast 35min.
  2. Add Sauce to Gnocchi, combine, and bake 25min.
  3. Scatter a generous helping of Vegetables on Gnocchi, mix through, and serve.

See also: Potato Farls

Chef's Notes

Try substituting the water you boil the potatoes in, with stock.
