Pita

Category: Bread

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ C Plain Flour
  • 1 t Yeast
  • 1 t Salt
  • ½ C Water, warm
  • 1 T Oil
  • ½ T Honey

Method

Prep Time: 1hr 40min

Temperature: 250+ with Baking Steel

Cook Time: 3-5min per

Servings: 4 Pita

  • 1 ½ C Flour
  • 1 t Yeast
  • 1 t Salt
 Combine Flour, Yeast, Salt in a bowl.
  • 1 T Oil
  • ½ T Honey
 Stir together Water, Oil, Honey.

Add Water mix to Flour mix, stir to combine into a sticky, shaggy dough.

Gather dough into one mass, cover and rise 45min.
Degas, and knead until smooth and elastic, appx. 3min.

The dough should still be a little sticky at this point.
  • Baking Steel
 Divide into 4pcs, form each into balls, dust with flour, and cover to rise 45min.

If cooking pita breads immediately after they rise, place Baking Steel into oven and bring up to max temperature, will take appx. 1hr before baking can begin.
Flip balls so that the skin that formed on the top, is now on their underside, and roll to appx. ⅛", should be appx. 6" round.
Transfer to the peel, then to the oven to bake at the oven's hottest temperature for 2-3min.

Watch pita breads closely, allow them to inflate completely and colour to a medium-brown before removing from the oven.

If eating hot, serve immediately, otherwise, place them under a cloth until cooled completely, then store in an container with a good seal until required.

Chef's Notes

Pitas are done cooking when they "sigh"

A moment after they fully puff up, reach their peak, then "sigh" as they deflate a little.

These extra few moments give the tops a chance to brown a little.
