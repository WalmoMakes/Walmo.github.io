Pizza (Weeknight)

Category: Bread

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 200g Strong White Flour, plus extra for sprinkling
  • 1 t Salt
  • 1 t Yeast
  • 180g Water
  • 1 t Oil, plus extra for greasing
  • Cornmeal, for scattering

Method

Prep Time: 1hr 45min

Temperature: 250+ with a Baking Steel

Cook Time: 3 ½ - 4min per

Servings: 2x 8" Pizzas

  • - 200g Flour
  • 1 t Salt
  • 1 t Yeast
 Combine Flour, Salt, Yeast in a large bowl.
  • 180g Water
  • 1 t Oil
  • Oil, for greasing
 Add Water, Oil to Flour mix, then stir to combine into a sticky mass.

Oil sides of bowl, above dough, then scrape down sides of bowl with bowl scraper, encouraging Oil to travel underneath dough.

Roll and tuck dough to form a single mass, allow to rise, covered, 45min.
  • Flour, for sprinkling
  • Baking Steel
 Remove dough from bowl, knead until elastic and absorbed all surface Oil, appx. 2min.

Cut dough into 2pcs (appx. 200g ea.), form each into approximate balls.

Place dough balls on a floured work-surface, sprinkle both with flour, and cover with a tea towel, then rise 45min.

Start preheating baking steel.
  • Flour, for sprinkling
 Very lightly sprinkle dough with flour, then gently shape one dough ball into a round by pressing with fingertips together to flatten base, forming crust, turning the dough frequently to maintain symmetry.

The aim is not to deflate the crust, while flattening and shaping the base.
  • Cornmeal, for scattering
 Scatter work-surface with Cornmeal, pick up and place down dough round on work-surface so that pizza underside contacts Cornmeal.

Form into an 8" round with gentle hand stretching, base should be thin, and crust appx. ¾" around.

Don't be afraid to pinch dough together to repair any small holes.
  • Cornmeal, for scattering
 Scatter pizza peel with Cornmeal, transfer pizza to pizza peel.

Quickly top pizza with sauce and toppings.

Test peel transferability by holding only peel handle, and shuffling pizza with firm but small circles; you should be able to see where, if anywhere, the pizza is still sticking, resolve this by scattering more Cornmeal underneath pizza.

Transfer to baking steel, bake 3 ½-4min at 250+, until crust is puffy and pale brown with leopard spotting.

Chef's Notes

If you remember to periodically knock-back the dough, you can store it portioned-up in tubs in the fridge for up to a couple days.

Don't be alarmed if it smells a little vinegary, that fermented dough flavour is quite pleasant when cooked.
