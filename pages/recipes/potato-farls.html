Potato Farls

Category: Miscellany

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 100g Potato, cooked, seasoned, mashed, cold
  • Seasoning: 1 T Butter, ½ t Salt
  • 25g Plain Flour, plus extra for dusting

Method

Prep Time: 5min

Temperature: Medium

Cook Time: 10min

Servings: 4 Quarters

  • 100g Potato
  • 25g Flour
  • 1 T Butter
  • ½ t Salt
 Add Potato, Flour in a bowl, if Potato unseasoned, season with Butter and Salt.

Combine into a soft dough ball.

Aim to not over-handle or overwork dough or it will become very sticky, very quickly.

Use a silicone spatula, if it helps you handle the dough less.
  • Flour, for dusting
 Using plenty bench Flour, roll 1cm thick, cut into a large round, so that it would fit in the base of a large pan.
Heat pan to medium heat, add round of dough into heated, dry pan.

Cook 3-5min per side, until both sides are golden-brown.
Transfer to wire rack to cool a little, cut into quarters.

Variations

Quick Farls: Replace Whole Potatoes with Dehydrated Potato Flakes, rehydrated.
