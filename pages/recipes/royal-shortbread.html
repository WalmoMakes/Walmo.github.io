Mum's Royal Shortbread

Category: Biscuit

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 140g Plain Flour
  • 50g Cornflour
  • Pinch of Salt
  • 115g Butter, softened
  • 60g Caster Sugar, divided

Method

Prep Time: 40min

Temperature: 180

Cook Time: 40min

Servings: 8-16 Biscuits

  • 140g Flour
  • 50g Cornflour
  • Pinch of Salt
 Combine Flour, Cornflour, Salt, in a bowl.
  • 115 Butter, softened
  • 35g Sugar
 In a second bowl, beat Butter until creamy.

Add Sugar to Butter, cream together until light and fluffy.
Add Butter mix to Flour mix and combine into a soft dough that barely holds together under pressure.

Press dough into a ball, wrap in clingfilm, and refrigerate 30min.
On a floured work-surface, roll dough into a rectangle, appx. 4x6x¾".

PreScore the dough slab into a 3x3 grid of rectangles using the back of a knife.

Dock dough with a fork 3-4 times per rectangle.
Transfer to a baking paper lined baking sheet.

Bake at 180 for 40min, until lightly golden on top.
  • 25g Sugar
 Sprinkle with remaining sugar, and allow to cool a little, appx. 15min, on baking sheet.

Transfer to a cutting board while still warm and cut into 8 or 16 pcs.

Allow to cool completely, appx. 30min

Variations

Shaped Biscuits

  1. Rather than process as in Stage 4, roll dough to either ¼" or ½" thickness, and cut out shapes.
  2. Bake at 180 for 30-35min, until lightly golden on top.

Moulded Biscuits: 55g

  1. Rather than process as in Stage 4, press dough into an oven-safe mould (like a Petticoat), then flatten the surface that would become their base.
  2. Bake depending on their thickness.

Empire Biscuit (Shortbread): Seedless Raspberry Jam, Glacé Cherries, Drizzle Icing

  1. Roll to ¼" thickness, cut into 2 ½" rounds with cutter, and bake at 180 for 30-35min.
  2. Once cooled, make the "Jam Sandwich" variant with Raspberry Jam, glaze with a simple Drizzle Icing, then top each with a Glacé Cherry.
  3. See also Shrewsbury Biscuit

Walnut Shortbread: 55g

  1. Add with Flour and Zest.

Chef's Notes

The only way to enjoy shortbread warm, is fresh. But be warned, too fresh out the oven, and they won't have set yet!
