Garden Centre Café Scones

Category: Cake

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 400g Self-Raising Flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 70g Butter, 1cm cubes
  • 10g Baking Powder
  • 210ml Milk

Method

Prep Time: 15min

Temperature: 200 Steamed

Cook Time: 22min

Servings: 12 Large Scones

  • 400g Flour
  • 70g Butter, 1cm cubes
  • 10g Baking Powder
 Rub Butter into Flour, until fine breadcrumb texture.

Add Baking Powder, and mix.
  • 210ml Milk
 Gradually add Milk to Flour mix, combine until smooth, soft dough.
  • 2-3" Pasty Cutter
 Knead gently, gently roll to 1 ½" thick, and cut rounds with cutter.

Cut straight down, do not twist, and transfer to baking tray.

If glazing or washing, brush only top with mixture.
  • Steamed Oven
 Bake in a steamed oven at 200 for 22min until light brown.

Variations

Cheese: 140g Mature Cheddar, Grated, divided, ½ t Salt, Pinch of Pepper, 1 t English Mustard

  1. Add 115g Cheddar, Salt and Pepper to breadcrumbed Flour and Butter mixture.
  2. Whisk Mustard into Milk.
  3. Roll dough to 1” thick, rather than 1 ½”.
  4. Once cut, wash tops with Egg and Milk mix, sprinkle with remaining 25g Cheese.
  5. Bake 16-18min.

Fruit: 60g Caster Sugar, 70g Sultanas

  1. Rehydrate Sultanas in boiling water 15min.
  2. Add Sugar and rehydrated Sultanas to Flour and Butter breadcrumb mixture.

Be-Ro's Rock Buns: 200g Sugar, 200g Butter cut into 1cm cubes, 200g Rehydrated Currants, 1 Egg, 2 T Milk

  1. Add Sugar and Butter in Stage 1.
  2. Add Currants to flour breadcrumb mixture.
  3. Whisk Milk into Egg.
  4. When combined, dough will be wet.
  5. Spoon loaded T mixture onto lined baking tray.
  6. Bake 15-20min, until golden-brown.
