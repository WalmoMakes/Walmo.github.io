Shortcrust Pastry

Category: Pastry

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 1 C (130g) Plain Flour, plus extra for dusting
  • ⅓ C (75g) Butter or Shortening, 1cm cubes
  • ½ t Salt
  • 2-4 T Water, cold

Method

Prep Time: 35min

Temperature: 190

Cook Time: 15-20 or 30-35min

Servings: 1 Lidded Pie or 24 Tarts

  • 1 C Flour
  • ⅓ C Butter, 1cm cubes
  • ½ t Salt
 Rub Butter into Flour and Salt, until breadcrumb texture.

Depending of style of crust, butter lumps remaining in breadcrumbed mixture can be large (for flaky pastry), or fully incorporated with the flour.
  • 2-4 T Water, cold
 Add Water, 1 T at a time, mixing after every addition, until dough forms.
Form into a ball, wrap in clingfilm, and refrigerate 20min.
  • Flour, for dusting
 Lightly flour rolling pin and work-surface, roll to desired thickness (appx. ⅛").

Prepare in desired fashion, pie, or tarts.
Bake at 190: Pies for 30-35min, Tarts for 15-20min.

Variations

Simple Sweet Shortcrust: No Salt, 2 t Granulated Sugar

  1. Replace Salt with Sugar in Stage 1.

Lite Cobbler: Replace Plain Flour with Self-Raising.

Cheese Twists: 1 C Grated Sharp Cheddar or Parmesan, Flake Salt

  1. Either combine Grated Cheese into dough then sprinkle rolled-out dough with Flaked Salt, or sprinkle both Cheese and Salt liberally over rolled-out dough and press-in gently with a rolling pin.
  2. Roll dough ⅛" thick, and cut into strips appx. ½" wide.
  3. Twist strips into long helixes, and place on a lined baking tray.
  4. Bake for 8-12min, until golden brown.
  5. Allow to cool on tray before decanting.

Blind Bake: Baking Beans

  • For use if filling is particularly wet.
  1. Grease dish, lay pastry and trim, as usual.
  2. Fill base of pie or tarts with baking beans, and bake at 190: pies 10-15min, tarts 5-7min, until stiff, dry, and light-brown.
