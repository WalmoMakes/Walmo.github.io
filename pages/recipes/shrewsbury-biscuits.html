Mum's Shrewsbury Biscuits

Category: Biscuit

Status: Tested

Ingredients

  • 140g White Sugar
  • 115g Butter, softened
  • 2 Egg Yolks
  • 225g Plain Flour
  • Zest of 1 Lemon

Method

Prep Time: 5min

Temperature: 180

Cook Time: 15min

Servings: 20 Biscuits

  • 140g Sugar
  • 115g Butter, softened
 Cream Sugar into Butter until pale.
  • 2 Yolk
 Add Yolks to Sugar mix, beat well to combine.
  • 225g Flour
  • Lemon Zest
 Sift Flour onto Sugar mix, add Zest, combine into a firm dough.
Knead lightly on a floured surface, until homogeneous.

Roll ¼" thick (or thinner), cut into 2 ½" rounds with a fluted cutter.
Brush excess flour from tops and underside of biscuits before transferring to a lined baking sheet.

Bake at 180 for 15min, until light brown.

Variations

Glazed: Glaze with a simple Drizzle Icing once biscuits cool.

Fruit Shrewsbury: 55g Dried Fruit, chopped

  1. Add with Flour and Zest.

Jam Sandwich: Reduce Sugar content from 140g to 120g

  1. Once baked and cooled, spread plenty fruit jam over one biscuit, and place another biscuit atop the jam, forming a sandwich.

Empire Biscuit (Shrewsbury): Seedless Raspberry Jam, Glacé Cherries, Drizzle Icing

  1. Roll to ¼" thickness, cut into 2 ½" rounds with cutter, and bake at 180 for 15min.
  2. Once cooled, make the "Jam Sandwich" variant with Raspberry Jam, glaze with a simple Drizzle Icing, then top each with a Glacé Cherry.
  3. See also Royal Shortbread
