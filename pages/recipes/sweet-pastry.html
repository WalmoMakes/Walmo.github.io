Sweet Pastry

Category: Pastry

Status: Tested

This dough makes great, tender bases for festive mince pies.

Ingredients

  • 165g Plain Flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 55g Caster Sugar
  • 25g Almonds, ground
  • 120g Butter, 1cm cubes
  • 1 Egg

Method

Prep Time: 45min

Temperature: 200

Cook Time: 15-35min

Servings: 1 Lidded Pie or 24 Tarts

  • 165g Flour
  • 55g Sugar
  • 25g Almonds, ground
 Combine Flour, Sugar, Almonds in a bowl.

Cut Butter into Flour mix, until breadcrumb texture.
  • 120g Butter, 1cm cubes
 Break Egg into Flour mix and combine into a soft dough.
  • 1 Egg
 Knead briefly on a lightly floured work-surface.

Shape into a ball, wrap in clingfilm, refrigerate for 30min.
  • Flour, for dusting
 Roll to ⅛”, line pie dish, or tart tray.

Bake at 200: large pie 30-35min, tarts 15min.
